Car owner questions

What is the minimum time?

One month. You can continue month by month after that.

Who drives my car?

A registered Waywe driver who rents the car from Waywe. Every driver's CNIC, licence and background are checked.

How do I get paid?

Monthly, by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa, after Waywe's commission.

Who pays for maintenance?

Waywe manages maintenance. The exact terms are written in your agreement.
