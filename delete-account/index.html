Delete your account

For the Waywe and Waywe Driver apps

You can delete your Waywe account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This works for both apps: Waywe (passengers) and Waywe Driver (drivers).

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Waywe or Waywe Driver app and log in.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are logged out.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't open the app, email admin@mywaywe.com with the subject "Delete my Waywe account". Send it from the email address you signed up with and include the phone number on your account. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (email, phone and password)
  • Your profile: name, role and student details
  • Your ride history, saved family contacts and ride packages
  • For drivers: your driver profile, vehicle details and uploaded documents
  • Your notification settings on your devices

What may be kept

Records that the law requires us to keep, such as payment records, may be kept for up to the period the law requires and are then deleted. They are not used for anything else.
