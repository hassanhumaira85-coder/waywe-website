Questions and answers

How do I book a ride?

Open the Waywe app, choose Short ride or Pick & Drop, set your pickup and drop-off on the map and tap Book. You see the fixed fare before you book, and you can follow the driver live.

How do I pay?

You can pay in cash, or online through JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer.

How is the fare worked out?

Each ride has a starting fare plus a rate per kilometre, depending on the vehicle. The app shows the full fare before you book.

Can women book rides with women drivers?

Yes. Choose a women-only ride and a verified woman driver will pick you up. Learn more.

How do route packages work?

If you travel the same route every day, buy a Weekly (7 rides), 15-day (15 rides, 8% off) or 30-day (26 rides, 15% off) package. Rides never expire. See packages.

How do I become a driver?

Download the Waywe app and choose Register as a driver. You will need your CNIC, driving licence and vehicle registration. Read more.

I own a car. Can I give it to Waywe?

Yes, for one month or longer. Waywe contracts a driver, collects the rent, deducts its commission and pays you a fixed monthly rental. See how it works.

I want to drive but don't have a car. What can I do?

Rent a car from Waywe for as long as you need it, and earn by taking rides. Rent a car to drive.

How can I contact Waywe?

Email us at admin@mywaywe.com. Once the app is live, you can also reach us from the Help section in the app.
