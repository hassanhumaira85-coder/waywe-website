Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Waywe ("Waywe", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use the Waywe mobile apps (Waywe and Waywe Driver) and the website mywaywe.com (together, the "Service"). Waywe provides short rides (including women-only rides), school/college/office pick & drop, and long-term car partnerships in Multan, Pakistan.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number and password (passwords are stored securely and we cannot read them).
  • Profile and role: whether you use Waywe as a passenger, student/parent, commuter or driver.
  • Booking information: pickup and drop-off locations, scheduled times, chosen service, vehicle, plan (weekly/monthly), number of passengers and notes you add.
  • Student details (Pick & Drop only): student name, student ID or roll number and class, entered by a parent or student when booking van service.
  • Location: for drivers, precise location while they are online or on a trip, so passengers and the Waywe team can track rides for safety. Passengers may share their location when booking or using safety features.
  • Driver information: name, phone, gender, CNIC (front and back), driving licence, vehicle details and documents uploaded for verification.
  • Driver selfies: drivers take a selfie when they register and, once a week at a random time while online, a new selfie. We use these photos only to check that the registered driver is the one driving. Selfies are stored privately, seen only by the Waywe team (and, if enabled, compared automatically by our face-matching provider), and are deleted when the driver deletes their account.
  • Family contacts: up to two names and phone numbers you choose to add, so you can send them ride updates on WhatsApp with a live tracking link. Anyone with that link can see the ride status and driver location until the ride ends; the link stops working after 2 days.
  • Ratings and reviews you give after a ride.
  • Technical information: basic device and app information needed to keep the Service working and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To receive, assign and complete bookings, and to connect passengers with drivers.
  • To show live ride status and driver location during a trip, and to provide safety features (ride sharing, SOS and emergency contact).
  • To verify and approve drivers, and to ensure women-only rides are served by women drivers.
  • To confirm fares, handle support requests and contact you about your bookings.
  • To improve the Service and keep it safe, and to meet legal obligations.

3. Sharing your information

  • With your driver or passenger: names, phone numbers, pickup/drop-off details and ride status are shared between the passenger and the assigned driver for that ride.
  • With the Waywe team (admins) to manage bookings, safety and support.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase to store data securely and Expo/Google services to run the app. They process data only on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Location data

The app asks for location permission. Drivers' location is collected while they are online or on an active ride, so trips can be tracked for safety. You can turn off location permission in your phone settings, but some features (such as accepting rides or live tracking) will not work without it.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secure servers with access controls, so users can only see their own bookings and the Waywe team can see what it needs to run the Service. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. Ride records may be kept for safety and accounting purposes.

7. Your choices and account deletion

  • You can view and update your profile information in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: open the Waywe or Waywe Driver app, scroll to the bottom of the home screen and tap Delete my account. Your account, profile, ride history, saved contacts and uploaded documents are deleted straight away.
  • If you can no longer open the app, follow the steps on mywaywe.com/delete-account or email admin@mywaywe.com from your registered email. We delete your data within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.

8. Children

Waywe accounts are created by adults. Students under 18 use the Pick & Drop service through a parent or guardian, who provides the student's details. We use student details only to organise safe school and college transport.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date.
