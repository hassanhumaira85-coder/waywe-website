Questions

Who can rent a car?

Registered and approved Waywe drivers with a valid CNIC and driving licence.

How long can I rent a car for?

For the period you need. The available periods and rent are shown when you choose a car.

Who pays for maintenance?

Waywe manages maintenance. Fuel and the driver's own costs are explained in the rental agreement.

Where do the cars come from?

From car owners who give their cars to Waywe for one month or more. Learn more.
